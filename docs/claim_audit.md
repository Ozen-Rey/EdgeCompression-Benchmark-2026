# Claim Audit

This audit records bounded wording for the current repository state. It is
intended to prevent overclaiming and to separate supported evidence from future
work.

| Claim | Supported by current evidence? | Evidence | Risk | Safer wording | Future work |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Router supports image/audio/video R-D-E rows. | Yes, within measured CSV/fixture contracts. | `DomainSpec`, multi-domain smoke coverage, real audio/video validation, router-ready CSVs. | Could be read as complete runtime execution support for all domains. | "The router can consume measured image/audio/video R-D-E rows through domain-specific contracts." | Broader domain-specific runtime execution and larger external validation. |
| Neural codecs occupy a distinct R-D-E region. | Supported in measured settings. | Image neural-inclusive oracle; video/audio measured summaries; energy and rate differences. | Could be generalized beyond measured hardware and datasets. | "In the measured settings, neural codecs often occupy different R-D-E regions from classical codecs." | More datasets, codec builds, hardware, and repeated energy measurements. |
| Neural selection is regime-dependent, not dominant across all settings. | Yes for current offline analyses. | Operational-regime simulation, neural-inclusive oracle, switch analysis. | Could be simplified into "neural is better" or "classical is obsolete". | "Neural selection is profile- and constraint-dependent in the measured analyses." | Broader regimes, applications, and hardware-aware calibration. |
| BD-rate is insufficient for energy-disjoint comparisons. | Supported as a methodological limitation. | R-D-E framing, energy-disjoint cases, non-overlapping operating regions. | Could be read as rejecting BD-rate in all contexts. | "BD-rate remains useful for overlapping R-D curves, but is fragile when energy and operating domains are disjoint." | Formal R-D-E indicators and literature-backed treatment of non-overlapping surfaces. |
| DomainSpec enables multi-domain routing. | Yes at schema/CSV contract level. | Built-in image/audio/video specs and CSV validation. | Could imply identical semantics across domains. | "`DomainSpec` enables domain-specific R-D-E CSV validation and router column mapping." | Richer domain semantics and multi-metric quality contracts. |
| Dataset/codec onboarding enables pluggability. | Yes for measured rows and explicit contracts. | `DatasetManifest`, ingestion, codec onboarding, full pluggability proof. | Could imply arbitrary datasets/codecs work without measurement effort. | "Onboarding enables measured dataset/codec rows to reach the router without router code changes when contracts are satisfied." | Independent external artifact evaluation and stronger template coverage. |
| Setup/doctor strengthens reproducibility. | Yes for router development environment inspection. | Router-only setup script, read-only doctor report. | Could imply full benchmark reproduction from a fresh checkout. | "Setup and doctor make the router development environment more inspectable; they do not install the benchmark stack." | Environment lockfiles, external tool manifests, and cross-machine doctor comparisons. |
| Energy measurements are hardware-dependent. | Yes and explicitly documented. | Energy provenance docs, system-aware reports, benchmark protocols. | Understating this could overstate transferability. | "Energy measurements should be interpreted with hardware, driver, thermal, and toolchain provenance." | Cross-machine calibration and uncertainty reporting. |
| Generated results should not be versioned as portable ground truth. | Yes as repository policy. | `.gitignore`, setup docs, licensing/third-party notices, generated-output boundary. | Could conflict with thesis-local generated artifacts. | "Generated outputs are local artifacts; source code and contracts are versioned, while benchmark outputs require provenance." | Artifact bundles with explicit hashes and provenance for selected releases. |
| The router is ready for operational service deployment. | No. | Current work is offline validation and research prototype tooling. | Overstates robustness, monitoring, API stability, and safety. | "The router is a research prototype with offline decision audit support." | Service API, monitoring, online feedback controls, latency budgets, deployment validation. |
| Audio/video content-aware routing is complete. | No. | Current audio/video validation uses measured rows and policy comparison, not full content-aware predictors. | Overstates domain coverage. | "Audio/video measured-row routing is validated; content-aware audio/video prediction remains future work." | Domain-specific feature extraction, predictors, and leakage-safe validation protocols. |
| External components are licensed by this repository. | No. | `NOTICE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, router-only setup boundary. | Legal and attribution overclaim. | "External datasets, codecs, models, checkpoints, tools, and generated outputs remain under upstream terms." | Per-component license verification when packaging optional artifact bundles. |
